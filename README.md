# GeoPulse: A Low-Cost Machine Learning Rockfall Prediction System

A machine-learning-based research project focused on dynamic rockfall prediction and early-warning support.

## Overview

GeoPulse explores the use of machine learning together with geological, environmental, and sensor-related data to identify conditions associated with potential rockfall events.

The proposed system is designed to support dynamic risk assessment and provide early-warning information for potentially hazardous areas.

## Research Objectives

- Develop a low-cost approach for rockfall prediction
- Analyze geological and environmental factors related to rockfall
- Apply machine-learning techniques for risk prediction
- Classify potential rockfall conditions into different risk levels
- Support early-warning and preventive decision-making

## Research Focus

The research considers factors such as:

- Rainfall and precipitation
- Slope characteristics
- Ground vibration
- Temperature and environmental conditions
- Geological properties
- Historical rockfall information
- Time-dependent changes in monitored data
